Hack the Water Graph · Use Case 4

How does valuing the financial, social and environmental costs of sewer works together change whether Waternet should defer, reline or replace a pipe?

1,804sewer sections, one advice each
153 · 441reline · replace now
991 tCO₂e of those works, priced at €116/t
The problem

Pipe decisions are made in disconnected checks.

28information sources
5risks judged by feel
0places carbon is counted

van Riel, TU Delft / STOWA 2016 · UC4 brief

Our approach

One model, one advice per pipe.

1Knowledge graphOne SPARQL query on the W-OTL graph: 1,804 pipes with material, size, depth
2Three valuesFinancial · social (closures, trees) · environmental (MKI, CO₂)
3Risk over timeCondition 1–6, Markov model, faster ageing on peat
4Advicedefer · reline · replace

Data: W-OTL graph (SPARQL) · Madaster API (NMD pipes & liners) · ecoinvent · TNO GeoTOP soil · Maximo + IFC · trees, addresses · street works (WIOR, Stadsregie, NDW) · Barendrecht unit costs

What lies underneath

Peat under the pipes makes them sink, and digging dearer.

491pipes on ≥ 2.5 m of peat and clay (TNO GeoTOP): life −20 %, digging +20 %
779on 1.5–2.5 m: life −10 %, digging +10 %
1open model: pipes and soil layers together in IFC 4.3 (IfcGeotechnicalStratum)

Soil from TNO GeoTOP v1.6 (100 × 100 × 0.5 m). Settlement effects are our assumptions.

What we found

Carbon shifts a few decisions. Cost and liner life shift many.

6.6%of pipes change advice between €0 and €2,000 per tonne CO₂
153 → 349relines: Dutch unit costs (Barendrecht) vs dense-urban costs (VMM)
15 → 212relines when a liner lasts 30 instead of 15 years
Limitations

Weakest inputs: cost and condition.

What we lacked
  • Real Maximo costs: the cost source changes how we intervene for 11% of pipes
  • Inspection scores: condition is simulated from age and material, so “act past 69% of expected life” already reproduces 84% of the advice
  • Failure records
What Waternet can plug in
  • Own project costs per diameter
  • NEN 2767 condition scores from camera inspections
  • Internal CO₂ price and relining policy
