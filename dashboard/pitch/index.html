The problem

Pipe decisions are made in disconnected checks.

Our approach

One model, one advice per pipe.

1Knowledge graphWaternet pipes, materials, BIM
2Three valuesFinancial · social · environmental
3Risk over timeCondition 1–6, Markov model
4Advicedefer · reline · replace

Data: W-OTL graph · ecoinvent · Maximo + IFC · trees, addresses, street works · VMM costs · NMD CO₂ price

What we found

Carbon alone rarely flips a decision. Cost and liner life do.

<1%of pipes change advice from €0 to €2,000 per tonne CO₂
15 → 301relines once real unit costs are used
Liner lifedecides reline vs replace, not the CO₂ price
Limitations

Weakest inputs: cost and risk.

What we lacked
  • Real Maximo costs for relining and replacing
  • Inspection scores (condition is simulated)
  • Failure records
