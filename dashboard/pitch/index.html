The proof · What it changes · 0:50

Pricing carbon does not make relining win. It makes replacing win.

1.6 vs 3kg CO₂e per metre per year of service: new concrete pipe (~70 yr) vs liner (~25 yr)
153 → 51relines as the CO₂ price rises from €116 to €2,000 per tonne
27%of pipes on thick peat: they age faster and cost more to dig up

The number Waternet can now know: the lifetime value, per pipe, of acting now instead of waiting a year, split into money, disruption and carbon.
