The story · Our idea · 0:20
We help Waternet decide, for every sewer section, whether to defer, reline or replace, by putting financial, social and environmental factors in one euro value and letting a risk model advise the outcome.
1Knowledge GraphOne SPARQL query · 1,804 pipes
2Three ValuesFinancial · Social · Environmental, in €
3Risk Over TimeCondition 1–6 · Markov model · Soil
4Advice + WhyPer pipe, in a browser and in IFC
The proof · Demo · 1:00

Our solution: for each pipe, an individual advice.

Every PipeGets its own advice: defer, reline or replace.

What the demo shows
  • Most pipes can wait: 1,210 of the 1,804 pipes in our demo area need nothing now.
  • Where action is needed, the model mostly advises replacing (441) over relining (153).
  • The demo covers part of Amsterdam. The same model is built to run on Waternet's whole network.

The proof · Under the hood · 1:00

What’s real, what’s assumed, what’s missing.

What’s real
  • Network: W-OTL graph (SPARQL), NMD pipes & liners
  • Surroundings: GeoTOP soil, BAG addresses, trees
  • Street works: WIOR, Stadsregie, NDW
  • Environmental impact: ecoinvent MKI
What’s inferred or synthetic
  • Condition and failure risk: classes 1–6 simulated from age and material; failure rate per class is an assumed table
  • Unit costs: published Barendrecht 2023 prices
  • Closure days and peat settlement: estimated
What doesn’t work yet
  • Costs decide how, not whether: switching cost source changes 11% of advice
  • Needed: Maximo costs, inspection scores, failure history

The proof · What it changes · 0:40

Change the data and the advice moves. Environment is now part of the conversation.

Carbon costs moreRaise the CO₂ price and the advice shifts, pipe by pipe, with the reason shown for each.
The street opens anywayWhen other works are planned there, digging and closure are shared, so replacing becomes cheaper.
A busy streetMore homes nearby make each day of closure cost more, which favours the quicker reline.

For every pipe: what acting now instead of waiting a year is worth, in money, disruption and carbon.
